# Marketing Plan: Validate, Then Grow

Goal for the next 8 weeks: prove (or disprove) that families outside our own will **put records in, come back, and pay**. Growth spending only starts after that.

## 1. Positioning

**One line:** Every report, prescription and BP reading for your whole family, on one phone screen, ready when the doctor asks.

**Hero story (use it everywhere):** The doctor asked for a blood test. We had done the same test two months earlier and forgot. We paid twice. Med Vault exists so that never happens again.

**Who we target first (in order):**

| Segment | Why | Where they are |
|---|---|---|
| Adult children (28 to 45) managing parents' health | Feel the pain weekly, own Android phones, carry the files to clinics | Instagram, YouTube Shorts, family WhatsApp groups |
| NRIs and out-of-city children | Cannot be at the appointment, want parents' records on their phone | LinkedIn, Reddit (r/india, r/nri), NRI Facebook groups |
| Caregivers of chronic patients (diabetes, BP, thyroid, kidney) | Track readings and repeat tests every few months | Disease support groups, diabetes/BP communities |

Do not target young singles, iPhone users (Android PWA only today) or doctors as users.

## 2. What to claim, and what not to

| Claim | Use it? | Say it this way |
|---|---|---|
| No signup | **Yes, lead with it** | "No phone number. No email. No OTP. No spam calls." Indians are tired of giving their number to every app. |
| Anonymous | Careful | "We don't ask who you are." Avoid "anonymous": the photos of reports still contain names. |
| Complies with Indian health record regulations | **No** | The DPDP duties are covered, including the one-year audit log ([COMPLIANCE.md](./COMPLIANCE.md)), but data may be stored outside India and nothing is legal-reviewed yet. India has no health-record certification to claim. An unprovable "compliant" claim invites an ASCI complaint and kills trust if questioned. Say: **"Built around India's new data protection law (DPDP): you own your data, export it anytime, delete it in one tap."** Upgrade the claim only after a lawyer review. |
| AI reads prescriptions and reports | Yes | Show it, don't say it. It is the most "wow" moment on camera. |
| Visit brief on WhatsApp | **Yes, core demo** | The doctor-facing payoff of the whole app. |
| Lab trends and BP charts | Yes | "See if mom's sugar is actually improving." |
| Medicine conflict questions | Yes, carefully | "Questions to ask your doctor", never "detects dangerous interactions". Medical claims are a legal risk. |
| Works offline | Yes | Clinic basements and hospital corridors have no signal. Real pain. |
| Activity log (Settings → Activity) | **Yes, trust proof** | "See every phone that opened or changed mom's records. Lost a phone? Remove it in one tap." Concrete proof beats the word "secure". |
| Free | Yes, with "for early families" | Sets up the paid tier without a bait-and-switch feel. |

**The weakness we must address upfront:** no signup means a lost phone plus a lost recovery key loses the vault. Every onboarding video ends with "save your recovery key and link a second phone". Turn it into a feature: "Link your brother's phone too. Both of you see mom's records."

## 4. Validation plan (weeks 1 to 8)

### What "the market is ready" means: set the bar before launch

| Signal | Measures | Pass bar |
|---|---|---|
| Activation | Vaults with at least 1 member and 3 records in week 1 | 40% of created vaults |
| Retention | Vaults with a device seen 30 days after creation | 25% |
| Family pull | Vaults with 2+ linked devices | 20% (strongest signal: they shared it) |
| Depth | Median records per retained vault at day 30 | 15+ |
| Willingness to pay | "Join Pro" taps or pre-payments (see below) | 5% of active vaults |
| Organic | New vaults not traced to your own posts | Any steady trickle by week 6 |

Miss activation: onboarding problem, fix the product. Miss retention: pain is not frequent enough, reposition around chronic care. Miss willingness to pay: keep free, look at B2B (clinics, labs, insurers).

### Measure without breaking the privacy promise

No tracking scripts. The promise is "we don't know who you are"; Google Analytics would break it. Use Cloudflare Web Analytics (cookieless) for the landing page, plus weekly aggregate counts straight from D1:

```sql
-- vaults created per week
SELECT strftime('%Y-%W', created_at) wk, COUNT(*) FROM vaults GROUP BY wk;
-- 30-day retention: vaults older than 30 days with a device seen in the last 7
SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT v.id) FROM vaults v JOIN devices d ON d.vault_id = v.id
WHERE v.created_at < datetime('now','-30 days') AND d.last_seen_at > datetime('now','-7 days');
-- family pull: vaults with 2+ devices
SELECT COUNT(*) FROM (SELECT vault_id FROM devices GROUP BY vault_id HAVING COUNT(*) >= 2);
```

Add a `?src=` tag to every link (reel, LinkedIn, WhatsApp) and store only the source name on the vault at creation, so you know which channel works. Mention it in the privacy notice.

### Talk to users: the cheapest research

- Add one optional line in Settings: "Talk to the founder on WhatsApp" (opt-in, their choice to reveal a number).
- Do 15 calls of 15 minutes. Ask: When did you last open it? What were you doing? What almost made you stop? What would you pay for? Never ask "would you use this", ask about the last time.

## 5. Distribution ideas beyond ads

- **WhatsApp is the channel.** Make the Visit brief text end with "Made with Med Vault: [link]". Every doctor visit becomes an ad shown to a doctor and family. Biggest free lever we have.
- **Apartment/RWA groups and senior citizen clubs.** Offer a 20 minute "organise your parents' medical files" session. Set up vaults on the spot.
- **Diagnostic labs and pharmacies** later: "Save this report to Med Vault" QR on report envelopes. Labs gain repeat customers who track trends.
- **Insurance season (Jan to March, tax-saving 80D)** and **claim time:** content on "how to keep records so your claim isn't rejected". Pair with a one-tap "Insurance pack" export later.
- **Festivals and visits home (Diwali, summer):** "When you go home this Diwali, spend 30 minutes putting your parents' reports in Med Vault." Strong NRI and migrant-children hook.

## 6. Paid tier hypotheses (test, don't build yet)

| Candidate | Why people may pay |
|---|---|
| More storage and unlimited AI scans | Heavy users with years of reports |
| Insurance claim pack (PDF bundle by date range) | Directly saves money and stress |
| Shareable doctor link (time-limited, read-only) | Second opinions, emergencies, specialists in another city |
| Caregiver alerts (BP above limit, missed medicine) to a remote child | NRI segment; highest willingness to pay |
| Priority support and assisted import ("send us your file, we digitise it") | Older users, busy families |

Price anchor: less than one repeated blood test. "₹499 a year. One repeated CBC costs more."

## 7. Product gaps that will hurt marketing (fix before scaling)

1. **"Have I done this test?" in one tap.** The hero story promises it. Search works, but a test-name lookup from the home screen with "last done: 12 Aug, result: normal" would make the demo land in 2 seconds.
2. **Recovery anxiety.** Nudge to link a second phone on day 1, not only show the key. Settings → Activity already lets families remove a lost phone; say so in onboarding.
3. **Hindi interface.** The parents' generation is the real end user.
4. **iPhone.** NRIs skew iPhone. Confirm the PWA works on iOS Safari before targeting them, or exclude them from that campaign.
5. **Trust page:** who runs this, where data lives, how it's protected, in plain words, with a screenshot of the Activity log. "No signup" from an unknown name raises "who's behind this?" Show your face and name.
