Questions

Before you start

Is it really free?

Yes, free for early families. AI photo reading has a fair daily limit per family; everything else is unlimited.

Why no phone number or email?

Because we don't need them, and you are tired of spam calls. Your phone itself is the key. Add more phones with a one-time code, and keep the recovery key somewhere safe.

What if I lose my phone?

From any other linked phone, remove the lost one in Settings and it loses access straight away. Lost every phone? Open the vault on a new phone with your recovery key. That's why we ask you to link a second phone and save the key on day one.

Who can see our records?

Only the phones linked to your vault. Every change is listed in Settings → Activity. Vaults never see each other.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. It runs in the browser on any phone. To add it to your home screen: on Android, tap “Install” or the menu → Add to Home screen; on iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

Is this medical advice?

No. Med Vault keeps your records and suggests questions to ask. Your doctor decides everything. Never stop or change a medicine without asking them.

Can I get my data out?

Anytime. Settings → Backup downloads every record, photo and PDF, with a page that opens in any browser.
